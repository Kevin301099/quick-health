How a brand gets on our shelf

Six gates.Most productsstop here.

Startups and stock-exchange-listed companies go through exactly the same gates. Size and reputation don't buy a shortcut, and neither does money: the verification fee is the same whether a product passes or fails.

A product that fails any gate goes back to the brand with the exact reason and a way to fix it.

  1. 1

    Company and licence checks

    Incorporation, GST and trademark ownership, plus the FSSAI licence verified on FoSCoS, product liability insurance, and a disclosure of every regulatory action in the last three years.

    • FSSAI
    • GSTIN
    • MCA
    • Insurance
  2. 2

    Facility audit

    Food-safety certifications, the latest third-party audit, and a live video walkthrough of the kitchen or plant. High-risk categories get a physical visit.

    • FSSC 22000
    • HACCP
    • GMP
  3. 3

    Product dossier

    Every ingredient, including what is inside each ingredient. Every claim on the pack, with its evidence. A certificate of analysis for the current batch.

    • Sub-ingredients
    • Claims
    • CoA
  4. 4

    Ingredient and claim review

    Automatic screening against our banned list and nutrition limits. Then a food technologist checks every claim against FSSAI's rules on claims like “natural” and “no added sugar”.

    • Banned list
    • Nutrition limits
    • FSSAI claims
  5. 5

    Independent lab test

    We send samples to NABL-accredited labs ourselves. Protein is checked for amino spiking, honey for sugar syrup, spices for ethylene oxide, produce for pesticide residues.

    • NABL
    • Heavy metals
    • Residues
  6. 6

    Taste panel

    Healthy food has to be good food. If our panel wouldn't buy it again, it doesn't go on the shelf.

    • Blind tasting

Blind testing

We shop ourown shelves.In secret.

A brand's own lab certificate gets a product in the door. Keeping it on the shelf depends on the packs we buy back when nobody is expecting it.

  1. 1Bought anonymously

    Random packs are pulled from our own dark stores. The brand isn't told.

  2. 2Tested independently

    Sealed samples go to NABL-accredited labs with a chain-of-custody record.

  3. 3Published, pass or fail

    Results appear on the product page with the date, the lab and the batch.

If a batch ever fails

You hear it from us first.

  1. Minute 0

    A result comes back outside our limits.

  2. Within 1 hour

    That batch stops selling in every store and warehouse, with one tap.

  3. Same hour

    Everyone who bought that batch gets a WhatsApp message and an SMS.

  4. Automatically

    Your money goes back to your original payment method. No forms, no calls.

  5. Afterwards

    We publish what failed, what we did and what the brand must fix.
